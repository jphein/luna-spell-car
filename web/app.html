Luna's Spell Car ✦

Pick a magic game!

For grown-ups

Window Magic: Luna taps blue or red when she spots that color outside; purple gives one of each (blue + red = purple, like her deck). Cards she can afford glow; colored symbols need that color, gray numbers take any color, just like the real game.

Make a Creature: she designs her own creature card. Stronger creatures cost more. Saved creatures appear in Window Magic, Count the Mana, and Who Wins.

Story Time asks Claude to write a short, gentle story starring the creatures she picks, then reads it aloud (it uses your Claude account, so it asks permission the first time). Memory Match is a flip-and-find pairs game. In My Spellbook, tap one of her creatures to edit it, and use Print my creatures to get a real-card-size PDF to cut out and sleeve.

Rootha Says: Simon Says, read aloud, no screen needed. You're the judge: if she moves when Rootha didn't say it, gotcha! Every correct answer earns a star, and every 10 stars earns a sticker in My Spellbook. Tap any question to hear it again. Everything is saved on this device only.

Mana Bubbles: pop bubbles to pay a card's cost; colored slots need that color, gray slots take any. Treasure Catch: slide a basket to catch Goldspan Dragon's falling coins and gems (30 seconds, nothing to lose). Game Night is a life counter (20 or 40 life, 2–4 players) with dice, a coin, and "who goes first." In Make a Creature she can draw her own picture, and Art Studio is free painting with a saved gallery. With a Spell Car server (below), the tablet app also gets Story Time, AI creature pictures, and natural neural voices from your Azure account. Magic movies (Google Veo, via the same server) let her cards and creatures move: tap a card in My Spellbook, or use "Make all deck cards move" below; each clip is made once, shared by all your devices, and plays offline. Magic School turns the printed Spellbook into twelve spoken, tappable lessons (zones, card parts, mana, the turn, combat, prowess and magecraft, Treasure, Rootha's Elemental trick, and a full practice turn), ending in a printable diploma. The Star Map holds twelve harder treasures (long streaks, high scores, collecting every card) that light up a dragon constellation. Levels come from every star she has ever earned, so spending stars in the Star Shop never lowers her level or stickers. There are nine hidden secrets; the ❓ tiles in My Spellbook give hints, and finding them all opens the hidden Secret Garden colors. Backup in My Spellbook moves her creatures and stars between the tablet and the web version.
